- "A single leaf with no piece of stem may root, but it will not grow into a new plant, because new shoots grow from the bud found at a node."
- "Find the nodes. Look for the small brown bumps or stubby roots on the stem opposite each leaf. Those are the nodes."

- "Stand the cuttings in it so the nodes are under the water and the leaves stay above it."
