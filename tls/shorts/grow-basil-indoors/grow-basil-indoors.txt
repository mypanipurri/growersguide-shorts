Where each fact comes from on the page (re-fetched 2026-11-25):
- "Left alone, basil grows one tall stem and then flowers. Pinching changes that. Each time you remove a stem tip just above a pair of leaves, the two buds at that point grow into new stems. Minnesota Extension says new growth should show at the cut within about a week."
- "Start early. When three or four leaf pairs have formed, pinch or snip off the top pair along with the growing tip."
- "Cut just above a leaf pair, not in the middle of a bare stem."
- "Repeat on each new stem once it has grown a couple more pairs of leaves."
- "Harvest the same way. Every harvest is a pinch."
