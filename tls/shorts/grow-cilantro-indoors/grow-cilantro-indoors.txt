Title:
Cilantro Keeps Bolting? Sow a New Pot Every Few Weeks

Description:
Cilantro is a cool-season plant, and warmth plus long days push it to flower, so every pot bolts eventually. University of Wisconsin–Madison Extension gives a new sowing every 2 to 3 weeks, so the next pot is ready when the last one flowers.
